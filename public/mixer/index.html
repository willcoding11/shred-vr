Grind mixer

The grind

This is the rail sound the game plays, running live. Move a slider and you hear it immediately — no reload, no riding to a rail to find out. It is the one sound in the project with nothing real to check it against, so it has to be dialled in by ear.

Conditions

The wind and the board rumble ride along underneath at whatever speed you set, so you are hearing the grind in the mix it actually arrives in — not on its own.

One-shots

The knocks either side of a grind, and the rest of the game's hits, for comparison — a clack that sounds wrong next to a crash is worth knowing about.
